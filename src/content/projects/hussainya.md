# Case study, shown on this project's own page at /work/<slug>/.
problem: >-
  Every Muharram, the month our community gathers most nights, it was the
  same: three group chats, a photo of a flyer someone took off a
  noticeboard, and my mum asking which centre had something on that night.
  Nobody could answer without ringing someone. The prayer app on my phone
  used a calculation we do not follow, and the date it gave for the
  religious calendar was a day off what our centre had announced. A couple
  of community directories list the centres here, but they are partial,
  years out of date, and none of them tell you what is on tonight. So the
  practical part of the week ran on screenshots.

approach: >-
  I decided the calculated answer is not automatically the right one. Prayer
  times follow the method our community uses, which puts the evening prayer
  well after sunset rather than at it, and every time can still be nudged by
  up to an hour so it matches what your own centre calls out. The calendar
  works the same way: when the community sights the new moon a night later,
  I shift the whole thing by a day for everyone. I would rather agree with
  your centre than be technically correct on my own.

next: >-
  It is in private beta. Testers have it; both app stores still say coming
  soon. The directory is seventy-odd centres, and keeping it right is the
  part that never finishes. The last batch I added was forty-four, each one
  checked against the centre’s own web page, and five more were thrown out
  because I could not confirm they were real, distinct centres rather than a
  name on someone else’s list. Two sections did not make the first release.
  The next real test is whether centres post their own events instead of me
  chasing them.

capabilities:
  - title: "Seventy-odd centres across Australia"
    detail: >-
      Address, phone, website and socials for each one, plus what they have
      coming up. Grouped by state with yours at the top, and searchable by
      name or suburb. Anyone signed in can propose a correction, and it
      comes to me to approve.
  - title: "Prayer times you can correct"
    detail: >-
      Calculated the way our community does it, which puts the evening
      prayer well after sunset. Each of the five can be nudged by up to an
      hour to match what your own centre calls. Six cities so far:
      Melbourne, Sydney, Brisbane, Perth, Adelaide and Canberra.
  - title: "A calendar that knows the occasions"
    detail: >-
      The religious year alongside the ordinary one: days of mourning in
      red, births in green, Eid in gold, and a dot on any day a centre you
      follow has something on.
  - title: "Host a gathering, share one link"
    detail: >-
      Create a private event at your own address and send the link. Whoever
      opens it and accepts gets it in their calendar inside the app, and it
      never appears in the public listings.
  - title: "The annual tithe, worked out in front of you"
    detail: >-
      Khums is a fifth of what you have left over at year’s end, split
      between two funds. Enter your surplus and your deductions and every
      line of the working stays on screen, including what you have already
      paid.
  - title: "Counters that keep your place"
    detail: >-
      Say how long you have been behind on prayers and it sets a starting
      count for each of the five, which you tick down as you catch up. The
      dhikr counter beeps at the number you set, so you can keep your eyes
      shut.

Hussainya gathers the practical side of Shia community life into one calm
place. Instead of chasing events through group chats and screenshots, you browse the
Islamic centres across Australia, follow the ones you attend, and their events
fill a calendar beside the month's occasions. Prayer times, the direction to
pray, and counters for the everyday things sit in the same app, alongside home
screen widgets for the next prayer and today's events.
